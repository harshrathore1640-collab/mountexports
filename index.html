GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Useful information for natural stone buyers and project partners.

Which natural stone categories does Mount Exports deal in?

Mount Exports deals in Indian Marble, Granite, Patagonia and other Exotic Colours, with a product range that includes Titanium Gold, Cosmic Black, Titanium Black and selected Rajasthan stones.

Does Mount Exports have its own quarry?

Yes. Mount Exports has its own quarry for Titanium Gold in Rajasthan, supporting direct sourcing and selective block grading.

Can I enquire about both blocks and finished slabs?

Yes. Share the stone name, preferred format, approximate quantity and project requirements with the team so your enquiry can be reviewed.

Can you discuss international export requirements?

Yes. International buyers can share their destination country, required quantity or container details, dimensions and finish preferences through the enquiry form.

How can I request product images, details or a quotation?

Use the enquiry form or contact Mount Exports directly by phone, email or WhatsApp. Mention the product name and any specifications you already have.
